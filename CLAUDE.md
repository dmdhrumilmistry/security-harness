## The one rule that is easiest to break

**Never use em dashes or en dashes. Use a plain ASCII hyphen (`-`).**

This covers Markdown, code comments, commit messages, PR titles and bodies, YAML
comments, and anything written into the `sh-kb-*` knowledge bases. Where an em dash
would fit, use a spaced hyphen (` - `), a comma, a colon, parentheses, or two
sentences.
